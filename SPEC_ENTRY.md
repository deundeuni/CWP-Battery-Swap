## 1. Overview
CWP-Entry is a guided entry and position alignment system designed for EV battery swapping and general-purpose mobility alignment. It targets ±2mm precision alignment via a 3-stage alignment process (Guide -> Entry -> V-Home) utilizing overhead line lasers and bottom V-rails.

---

## 2. Background & Minimal Infra Retrofit Economics

* **Minimal Infra Retrofit:** Instead of constructing expensive new dedicated robotic facilities or performing disruptive full-floor demolition, this system reuses 20+ year field-proven car-wash V-rail and chain-pull mechanisms already deployed across nationwide gas stations, parking lots, and car washes. By integrating 5cm (SLIM) and 8cm (SHUTTLE) modules onto existing infrastructure surfaces with minimal retrofitting, it achieves battery swap station deployment at the lowest possible CAPEX.
* **High-Skill Human-Centric Integration:** Rather than replacing or monitoring on-site technicians with intrusive AI, Quiet Assist haptic feedback (1x/2x) directly integrates and leverages the existing maintenance skills and field know-how of skilled engineers, treating practical human experience as the primary decision-making element.
* **Public Domain Synergy:** Eliminates corporate patent monopolies and royalty fees by combining 100+ year proven standard mechanical elements (levers, differential gears, V/U/C/T grooves) while bypassing proprietary corporate technologies.

## 4. Lineup
- **SLIM (Infra-Transition Type):** 5cm flush mount/surface lay, single chain-pull drive, compatible infrastructure retrofit type.
- **SHUTTLE:** 8cm pallet, 4-point pneumatic lift.
- **FREE:** Combined single motor + 2-stage torque sensor + embedded pallet, LED guide + line laser guide.

---

## 5. Universal Mobility, Space & Safety Escape
- **Form-Factor Agnostic:** Extended beyond passenger EVs to support UAM/drone landing skids, logistics AGVs/AMRs, micro-mobility, lunar regolith exploration rovers, and orbital satellite payload/battery swap modules.
- **L0 HW Fail-Safe:** Triggers a 0.1ms HW Intercept signal (EN LOW) upon fire, power loss, or passenger entrapment to automatically vent pneumatics, release chain clutches, and allow unpowered mechanical detachment/external towing.
- **Rescue Clearance:** Guarantees side door clearance (up to 850mm) and automatically switches overhead line lasers to emergency illumination panels (30-min battery backup) during blackouts.
